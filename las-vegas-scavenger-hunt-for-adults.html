Las Vegas Scavenger Hunts for Adults: Your Strip Options, Compared

Yes. There is a scavenger hunt on the Las Vegas Strip for adults. City Clue is a self-guided, 21+ walking game on the Strip. It starts outside The Cosmopolitan, covers 13 stops over about 2.7 miles (4.3 km) in about 90 minutes, and costs $14.99 per player. Each player needs a ticket.

This page compares formats: a self-guided browser or app game, a hosted group hunt, and a free do-it-yourself list. It is a way to choose, not a ranking of every company in town.

Quick answer: the main kinds of Vegas scavenger hunts for adults

Self-guided app or browser games

A self-guided game suits a couple, a friend group, or a small party that wants to start on its own schedule. City Clue plays in your phone's browser. Other companies sell an app download and a route you follow on your own. You trade a live host for a simpler setup: book, open the game, and walk. You also keep an eye on heat, traffic, and your own pace. Read the listing for where it actually walks, because a "Las Vegas" hunt is sometimes Downtown, not on the Strip.

Hosted or private group hunts

A hosted hunt suits a corporate outing or a large party that wants someone else to run the clock. A producer or host meets the group, splits teams, and scores the game. You trade flexibility for a managed start, and the price is often a package for the group rather than a ticket per person. Very large events are often a better fit here than in a self-guided phone game.

Free DIY photo lists

A free list is a checklist you make or find: photo prompts, a few landmarks, no ticket. It suits a group that already knows the Strip and wants zero cost. You do the planning, and there is no route, host, or refund, because there is nothing to buy.

The route

The walk is about 2.7 miles (4.3 km), about 90 minutes, self-guided, with 13 stops. It starts outside The Cosmopolitan, 3708 S Las Vegas Blvd, at the main Strip entrance, outdoors. It finishes on a pedestrian bridge nearby, with The Cosmopolitan in sight.

What you actually do

You hunt for details, match a picture, answer true or false, play odd one out and rapid trivia, and hear stories between stops. Points add up. Hints are there if you need them, but they cost you. No show tickets are included.

Tips for playing a scavenger hunt on the Strip

If it is really hot, go in the morning or evening and bring water. A couple of stops include short indoor cut-throughs. Wear comfortable shoes. Take a charged phone with data and location services on. ID is a good idea. Mornings and evenings are coolest in summer. The Strip at night is very on-theme.

Pedestrian bridges cross the boulevard. Use them. Obey traffic signals and stay aware of your surroundings. Do not step into a street to chase a clue. The walk is mostly flat sidewalks and bridges, with elevators or escalators at major crossings. It has not been certified as wheelchair accessible.
