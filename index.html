What it is

Clues, stops, and the walk between them.

A route you play outside. Three pieces, kept simple.

  1. 01

    Clues

    A short prompt for the block you are standing on. It points you to the next stop: a direction, a detail, something you would only catch on foot.

  2. 02

    Stops

    Real places on the route. You show up, take in the spot, and pick up the next thread. The screen stays light so the street stays in front of you.

  3. 03

    Walking tour

    A start, a run of stops, and a last clue, paced for a walk you can finish. Built for adults who want a night out with a point to it.

Vegas first

The Strip pack opens before any other city.

Las Vegas comes first. The Strip pack is a night walk you play clue by clue, on the sidewalk, from one real stop to the next. Other cities can wait.

  • Play it on your phone, at your own pace
  • Written for adults
  • Long enough for an evening, short enough to finish
  • Independent of any casino, resort, or the City of Las Vegas
